`implementation-requirements.md`, and the local design and matching SVG.
Follow the requirements' repeatable-implementation guidance during Task 2;
do not turn Task 1 into deployment research.

Record resolved decisions and approvals in ignored `plans/` and reuse them
when resuming. Reopen only missing, conflicting, or materially changed choices.

preview are separate gates.

Reuse verified repository helpers and operation shapes before researching new
ones. Do not modify another lab's hashed deployment helper to implement Lab 3.
Keep workbook-specific mappings, names, settings, fixtures, and generated
solutions out of these shared instructions and the student README.

Preserve Lab 0 and other labs. Preflight names and shared connections, upload
only owned artifacts, and verify preservation without saving connection values.
Respect both identity boundaries and explicitly confirmed publisher exceptions.
Never modify any `iac/.gitignore`.

Render and test both payload-visibility modes before previewing deployment.
Report offline checks separately from live evidence and unexercised failures;
successful provisioning alone is not successful message processing.
